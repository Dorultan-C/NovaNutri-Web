## 2. What the Services do

Riov is a meal-planning and grocery app. Its main features are:

- **Recipe catalogue:** recipes with estimated cost per person and in total, nutrition, allergens, equipment, prep time and servings, available in several languages.
- **Cooking mode:** step-by-step instructions with photos and timers.
- **Weekly meal planner:** includes automatic plan generation, which runs on rules inside the App rather than AI.
- **Basket:** a shopping list built from your plan.
- **PDF export:** export your plan or shopping list as a PDF, or copy it as text.
- **Nutrition diary:** track calories and macros against a target.
- **AI food scanner:** photograph food or ingredients so the App can identify them and estimate nutrition.
- **AI translation:** recipe content and in-app announcements are translated by AI.
- **Reviews and photos:** rate recipes and review them, with photos.
- **Submitted recipes:** submit your own recipes, which are checked by moderators before they are published.
- **Favourites, dietary preferences, allergies and disliked ingredients**, used to filter recipes.
- **Notifications:** in-app and push notifications.
- **Feedback form:** you can attach screenshots or files.
- **Account options:** email, Google sign-in, or a guest account.
- **Supermarket price estimates:** prices taken from tracked supermarket data for your region.
- **Website:** information, a waitlist and a contact form.

## 4. Health and diet data (Article 9 GDPR)

Data about your allergies, intolerances, disliked ingredients, **diet type** (for example vegan or Mediterranean, which can reveal health conditions or beliefs), nutrition diary, meals logged from cooking mode, and food saved through the scanner or quick-add can reveal information about your health. We treat all of it as **special-category data**. We process it **only with your explicit consent** (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).

- **Opt-in before use.** We ask for this consent in a separate, explicit opt-in screen. It appears before you enter allergies, dislikes or a diet type, use the nutrition diary, log meals from cooking mode, or save scanner or quick-add results.
- **Optional.** Without consent, these features stay switched off. You can still use the rest of the App. The meal-plan generator then works without your personal filters and shows an allergen warning.
- **Manage or withdraw at any time** in **Menu › Health data & privacy**, or by emailing [info@riov.app](mailto:info@riov.app). When you withdraw, you can choose to:
  - **withdraw and delete:** your health and diet data is erased; or
  - **withdraw and keep data paused:** the data is kept but not processed in any way, until you consent again or it is deleted under the account retention rules in section 9.

  Withdrawal does not make earlier processing unlawful.
- **New consent.** We will ask for your consent again only if the way we process this data changes materially.
- **Other uses.** We do not use this data for advertising or for profiling with legal effects, and we never sell it.
- **AI providers.** We do not send your allergies, diet, diary or account identity to AI providers.

You can give, withdraw or delete your health data consent at any time in Menu › Health data & privacy.

## 9. How long we keep data

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Account, profile, plans, Basket, favourites | Until you delete your account, or until it is deleted for inactivity (below) |
| Health and diet data | Until you withdraw consent and choose deletion, or delete your account. Paused data is deleted under the same account rules |
| Guest accounts | Deleted after 12 months of inactivity |
| Inactive registered accounts | After 23 months of inactivity we send a warning email. The account is deleted at 24 months, and never less than 30 days after the warning. Signing back in cancels the deletion. Administrator accounts are exempt, and so are registered accounts without an email address because they cannot be warned |
| Scanner images | Stored in your account and deleted with it |
| Reviews and review photos | Deleted when you delete them or your account |
| Submitted recipes | Pending (unpublished) recipes are deleted with your account. Published recipes are kept so the catalogue and other users' plans keep working, but your name is removed |
| In-app feedback and attachments | Deleted with your account |
| Website contact form messages | 12 months |
| Website waitlist | Until you unsubscribe or the App launches, whichever comes first |
| Server logs | Up to 90 days |
| Backups | Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for up to 35 days and is then overwritten |

**Deletion.** Use "Delete Account" in Settings, or email [info@riov.app](mailto:info@riov.app). Manual deletion and automatic deletion for inactivity both fully remove:

- your database records (Cloud Firestore)
- your stored files, including scanner images, review photos and feedback attachments (Cloud Storage)
- your sign-in record (Firebase Authentication)

The only exceptions are published recipes (kept with your name removed), the backup period above, and anything we must keep by law. Where the LOPDGDD requires it (art. 32), such data is blocked and kept only to make it available to courts and authorities while legal claims remain possible.

## 10. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest provided by Google Cloud, Firebase security rules that limit each user to their own data, restricted admin roles, and API keys kept server-side. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the AEPD and, where required, you.

## 11. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data and receive a copy.
- **Rectification** of inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten").
- **Restriction** of processing.
- **Portability:** receive data you provided in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Objection** to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting earlier processing.
- **Not be subject** to decisions based solely on automated processing.
- **Digital rights under the LOPDGDD**, including leaving instructions about your data after death (art. 3 and art. 96).

To exercise a right, email **[info@riov.app](mailto:info@riov.app)** or use the in-app settings. We will reply within one month. This can be extended by two months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you. We may ask you to verify your identity. Exercising your rights is free.

**Complaints:** you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (**Agencia Española de Protección de Datos**, C/ Jorge Juan 6, 28001 Madrid, www.aepd.es) or to the supervisory authority where you live or work.

## 12. Minimum age

The legal minimum age to use Riov is **14** (art. 7 LOPDGDD). If you live outside Spain and your country sets a higher age of digital consent (for example 15 in France or 16 in Romania), that age applies. Below that age, we process your data only with the consent of a parent or guardian. To apply one rule everywhere, at sign-up we ask every user to confirm that they are **at least 16 years old, or have their parent or guardian's consent**. We do not knowingly collect data from children below the applicable age without that consent. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. Parents or guardians can contact [info@riov.app](mailto:info@riov.app).
